# Visual effects resources

- Liquid Glass JS: https://github.com/dashersw/liquid-glass-js/tree/78cb6ccb0b9987bb60a88b14ccbd13a9e6e8ab2a. MIT, copyright 2025 Armagan Amcalar. Shape/refraction GLSL adapted to a shared WebGL2 renderer; fewer blur samples and live site backdrop sampling replace the demo page snapshot. The shader also supports the preprocessed letter/round masks.
- Liquid Logo: https://github.com/paper-design/liquid-logo/tree/689bb38a1e0d5a6a8baf2d34847635eefde19994. PolyForm Shield 1.0.0 (https://polyformproject.org/licenses/shield/1.0.0/). Its original parseLogoImage Poisson/bevel algorithm is used offline to produce ARXYLVE and circle masks. The chrome/metal shader is not included: these masks are rendered as transparent glass, as requested.

Complete license texts ship at public/vendor/licenses/.
